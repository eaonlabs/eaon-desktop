# Marketing: Sign in with ChatGPT and provider logos are not headline features

While reviewing the 2026.6 release film (see
[[Eaon 2026.6 release film (Apple style): how it is built]]), the user flagged the
"Sign in with ChatGPT." and "Real provider logos." title cards with "this one is not a
feature, fix that". Both are listed under Added in the 2026.6 changelog, but they are
not to be presented as features in marketing.

What changed in the film:
- The models frame is now about local models built in (Eaon's own llama.cpp, nothing
  to install), Recommended models with their fit for the Mac, and effort following the
  model. The effort shot uses an on-device model, not a ChatGPT-signed-in state.
- The "And so much more" wall dropped "Sign in with Hugging Face", "Poe",
  "Real provider logos" and "Pane logos follow the CLI".

Treat account sign-ins and cosmetic logo work as polish, not selling points, unless
the user says otherwise. Related: [[Pets are not an official feature — keep them out of marketing]].
