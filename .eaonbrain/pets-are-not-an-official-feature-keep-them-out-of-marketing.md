# Pets are not an official feature (and are now deleted)

On 2026-09-28 the owner said **"we do not have any official pets"**. On
2026-09-30 the co-founder asked to delete them, and the code, Settings page,
README line, CHANGELOG entry and docs mentions are gone. See
[[Pets — sprites, moods and the desktop window]] for what was removed and
where the activity hook (used by Discord presence) went.

For the 2026.6 launch film (`~/Downloads/eaon-launch-video`) this already meant:

- no pet sprites anywhere (the montage's last beat is prompt caching instead:
  a Work reply's usage line "170.4k in · 3.4k out · 87% cached");
- the Settings → Pets nav item is stripped from every re-hosted settings screen;
- the collage card that showed the fox is now the computer-use run.

Don't bring pets back into marketing, store listings, screenshots or docs.
